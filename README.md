hey i was tired of minecraft hopper mods breaking farms while also needing it for other farms thats why i made a mod thats for 26.1.2 paper that fixes this by making hoppers faster in the chunk you do the command in!
the commands are here:
/hopperspeed set <itemsPerTick> makes hoppers in your current chunk move items faster. For example, /hopperspeed set 1 moves 1 item per tick. Vanilla speed is 0.125, the max is 8, and values at or below vanilla reset the chunk.
/hopperspeed get shows the current speed of hoppers in your chunk.
/hopperspeed reset puts your chunk back to normal hopper speed.
/hopperspeed list shows every boosted chunk and its speed.
